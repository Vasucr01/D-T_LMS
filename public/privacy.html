Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 20, 2026

1. Overview

At D&T Career Planners LLP ("DT Careers"), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us during course registration and enrollment.

2. Information We Collect

When you register for a course through our website, we collect the following information:

  • Personal Details: Full Name, Email Address, WhatsApp Mobile Number
  • Academic Details: College/School Name, Stream/Class, Specialization, Semester
  • Enrollment Details: Selected Course, Applied Promo Code, Registration ID
  • Transaction Metadata: Razorpay Order ID, Payment ID, Payment Date, Payment Status

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Processing your course registration and payment verification
  • Communicating class schedules, batch links, and updates via WhatsApp/Email
  • Issuing course completion certificates and placement assistance referrals
  • Maintaining academic and accounting records

4. Payment Card Security

We do not store or process debit card, credit card, net banking, or UPI credentials on our servers. All financial transactions are securely processed by Razorpay using industry-standard 256-bit SSL encryption.

5. Data Protection & Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party marketing companies. Your data is stored securely in encrypted administrative records and is only accessed by authorized DT Careers personnel.

6. Cookies & Analytics

Our website uses essential browser storage to remember session details and ensure a smooth enrollment workflow. No invasive tracking cookies are used.
